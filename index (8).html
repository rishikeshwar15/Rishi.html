BEST DISHES

  • 1. Vivanta Coimbatore
    Best Dish: Chettinad Chicken Curry – Traditional South Indian chicken curry rich in spices and coconut.
  • 2. Welcomhotel by ITC Hotels
    Best Dish: Malabar Prawn Curry – Juicy prawns cooked in a spicy coconut gravy, served with appam or steamed rice.
  • 3. Radisson Blu Coimbatore
    Best Dish: The Great Kebab Factory’s Seekh Kebabs – Tender kebabs grilled to perfection with North Indian spices.
  • 4. Le Méridien Coimbatore
    Best Dish: Grilled Lamb Chops – Succulent lamb marinated in herbs and grilled, served with mashed potatoes.
  • 5. The Residency Towers Coimbatore
    Best Dish: Fish Moilee – Mild coconut-based Kerala fish curry, a favorite at The Pavilion restaurant.
  • 6. Darza Luxury Resort
    Best Dish: Kongu Nadu Mutton Curry – A local favorite made with roasted spices and curry leaves.
  • 7. The Orbis Hotel
    Best Dish: Chicken 65 – Spicy deep-fried chicken bites, an all-time Coimbatore classic.
  • 8. Hotel Kiscol Grands
    Best Dish: Paneer Butter Masala – Creamy North Indian curry with soft paneer cubes, great with naan or roti.
  • 9. FabHotel Jansi Deluxe
    Best Dish: South Indian Thali – A wholesome platter with rice, sambar, rasam, poriyal, and payasam.
  • 10. FabHotel Karpagam
    Best Dish: Ghee Roast Dosa – Crispy dosa cooked with rich ghee and served with coconut chutney and sambar.
