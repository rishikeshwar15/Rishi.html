HISTORY

  • WWE (World Wrestling Entertainment) is a global professional wrestling organization that started in 1953 as the Capitol Wrestling Corporation (CWC), founded by Jess McMahon and Toots Mondt.
  • It became the World Wide Wrestling Federation (WWWF) in 1963 under Vince McMahon Sr. and was renamed the World Wrestling Federation (WWF) in 1979.
  • In 1982, Vince McMahon Jr. took over and transformed wrestling into a worldwide entertainment business with stars like Hulk Hogan and events such as WrestleMania.
  • During the 1990s, WWE reached its peak popularity in the Attitude Era, with legendary wrestlers like The Rock, Stone Cold Steve Austin, and The Undertaker.
  • After defeating rival companies WCW and ECW in 2001, it was rebranded as WWE in 2002.
  • The company continued to grow through eras like Ruthless Aggression and the PG Era, introducing stars like John Cena, Randy Orton, and Roman Reigns.
  • In recent years, WWE expanded globally, promoted women’s wrestling to main-event status, and in 2023 merged with UFC under TKO Group Holdings.
