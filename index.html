Lalithaa Jewellery

History of Lalithaa Jewellery

Lalithaa Jewellery was founded by Kiran Kumar, who began his journey in the jewelry business at the age of 7 by selling earrings made from his mother’s bangles. He opened his first official showroom in Chennai in 1983, which later expanded into one of South India’s largest and most trusted jewellery brands.

  • Early life and beginnings:

    Kiran Kumar came from a poor financial background and began selling handmade earrings in Chennai.

  • First showroom:

    The first Lalithaa Jewellery store was opened in Chennai in 1983 (some sources say 1985).

  • Growth and expansion:

    The brand expanded to major cities including Hyderabad and Tirupati. Today, Lalithaa Jewellery operates 56 stores across four southern states and one union territory.

  • Wholesale to retail transition:

    The company grew by shifting from wholesale to a strong retail presence with transparent pricing.

  • Brand identity:

    Kiran Kumar personally appeared in advertisements, promoting transparent pricing and value for money.

  • Record-breaking showroom:

    In 2017, the Vijayawada showroom became one of the world’s largest jewellery stores.

Net Worth

Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Private Limited (LJMPL) reported a net worth of ₹1,928.15 crore as of December 31, 2024, up from ₹1,310.95 crore as of March 31, 2023.

  1. Net worth (2024):

    ₹1,928.15 crore

  2. Previous net worth (2023):

    ₹1,310.95 crore

  3. Growth factors:

    • Strong revenue increase • Rising net profits • Store network expansion • High gold prices • Successful customer loyalty programs
