NETFLIX

Netflix is an American subscription video on-demand over-the-top streaming service. The service primarily distributes original and acquired films and television shows from various genres, and it is available internationally in multiple languages.

Launched in 2007, nearly a decade after Netflix, Inc. began its pioneering DVD-by-mail movie rental service, Netflix is the most-subscribed video on demand streaming media service, with 301.6 million paid memberships in more than 190 countries as of 2025. By 2022, "Netflix Original" productions accounted for half of its library in the United States, and the namesake company had ventured into other categories, such as video game publishing of mobile games through its flagship service. As of 2025, Netflix is the 18th most-visited website in the world, with 21.18% of its traffic coming from the United States, followed by the United Kingdom at 6.01%, Canada at 4.94%, and Brazil at 4.24%.

History and Net Worth

Netflix was founded on August 29, 1997, by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph in Scotts Valley, California. Originally a DVD rental service by mail, Netflix shifted to streaming in 2007, revolutionizing how audiences consume entertainment. As of 2025, Netflix’s estimated net worth exceeds $250 billion, reflecting its position as a leader in the global streaming industry.

BEST MOVIE OF NETFLIX

One of the best movies on Netflix is "The Irishman" (2019), directed by Martin Scorsese. It is an epic crime drama starring Robert De Niro, Al Pacino, and Joe Pesci. The film received critical acclaim for its direction, performances, and storytelling, earning multiple Academy Award nominations.

WORST MOVIE OF NETFLIX

One of the lowest-rated Netflix original films is "The Ridiculous 6" (2015), starring Adam Sandler. The movie was heavily criticized for its humor and storytelling, and it holds a very low rating on review aggregator sites.

IMDB GOATED MOVIE IN NETFLIX

The most highly rated movie available on Netflix, often considered "GOATED" on IMDb, is "The Shawshank Redemption" (1994). With an IMDb rating of 9.3/10, it is widely regarded as one of the greatest films ever made. The film stars Tim Robbins and Morgan Freeman and tells the powerful story of hope, friendship, and redemption inside Shawshank Prison.
