CAST AND HISTORY

AVENGERS CHARACTER PLAY

  • Iron Man – Tony Stark (Robert Downey Jr.)
  • Captain America – Steve Rogers (Chris Evans)
  • Thor – Thor Odinson (Chris Hemsworth)
  • Hulk – Bruce Banner (Mark Ruffalo)
  • Black Widow – Natasha Romanoff (Scarlett Johansson)
  • Hawkeye – Clint Barton (Jeremy Renner)
  • Spider-Man – Peter Parker (Tom Holland)
  • Doctor Strange – Stephen Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch)
  • Black Panther – T’Challa (Chadwick Boseman)
  • Captain Marvel – Carol Danvers (Brie Larson)

MAIN CHARACTER AND PLAY

  1. Iron Man (Tony Stark)
    Actor: Robert Downey Jr.
    History: A billionaire genius who built the Iron Man suit to save his life and later used his technology to protect the world. He is one of the founding members of the Avengers.
  2. Captain America (Steve Rogers)
    Actor: Chris Evans
    History: A World War II super-soldier enhanced by a special serum. He became a symbol of courage and leadership and was frozen in ice before joining the Avengers in modern times.
  3. Thor
    Actor: Chris Hemsworth
    History: The Norse God of Thunder and prince of Asgard. Wielding his magical hammer Mjölnir, he protects both Earth and his home realm from cosmic threats.
  4. Hulk (Bruce Banner)
    Actor: Mark Ruffalo
    History: A scientist who, after a gamma radiation accident, transforms into the powerful Hulk when he becomes angry. Despite his struggle to control the beast within, he is a key Avenger.
  5. Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)
    Actor: Scarlett Johansson
    History: A former Russian spy turned S.H.I.E.L.D. agent and Avenger. Skilled in hand-to-hand combat and espionage, she uses her past to fight for good.
  6. Hawkeye (Clint Barton)
    Actor: Jeremy Renner
    History: A master archer and marksman. Though he has no superpowers, his precision and bravery make him a valuable member of the Avengers team.
  7. Scarlet Witch (Wanda Maximoff)
    Actor: Elizabeth Olsen
    History: Possesses powerful magic and telekinetic abilities. Initially an enemy of the Avengers, she later joined them and became one of their strongest members.
  8. Vision
    Actor: Paul Bettany
    History: A synthezoid created by Ultron and powered by the Mind Stone. He possesses great strength, intelligence, and the ability to phase through objects.
  9. Black Panther (T'Challa)
    Actor: Chadwick Boseman
    History: The king of Wakanda, a hidden African nation with advanced technology. As Black Panther, he defends his people and fights alongside the Avengers.
  10. Spider-Man (Peter Parker)
    Actor: Tom Holland
    History: A young superhero bitten by a radioactive spider. With his spider-like abilities, he joins the Avengers to prove that even a teenager can be a hero.
